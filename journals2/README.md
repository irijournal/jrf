# Soft Chaos Paper Co.: The Thinkers, Epics, Art and Satire Collection

Twelve A5 journals (120 pages each; The Integral Notebook is 250 pages), as print-ready PDF and Word (DOCX). Fonts are embedded in both. In the Word files, writing pages (prompts, lists, tables, trackers, letters) are editable; diagram and activity pages (idea cards, shlokas, quizzes, maps, family trees, connect-the-dots, kolams) are full-page images so they print exactly as designed.

## The Freud Files

*a slightly suspicious journal about your mind, your dreams and your mother (kidding. mostly.)*

A playful, informative journal built around Sigmund Freud's big ideas: the unconscious, id, ego and superego, defence mechanisms, Freudian slips and dreams. Inside: illustrated idea cards, an iceberg of the mind, 24 dream logs, a dream bingo card, slip and defence spotters, free association games, couch-session prompts, quizzes, a Freud-or-fake round and honest notes on what modern psychology keeps and what it has left behind.
