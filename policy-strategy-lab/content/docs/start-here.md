# Policy & Strategy Lab
## Start Here: the complete course package

A 20-day, 40-hour intensive in structured problem-solving for Indian public policy. Each participant takes one real problem from a vague concern to a recommendation defended in front of a panel. This package contains everything needed to run it.

## How to prepare a first run

1. Read the Course Concept Note and the Decision Log. Settle the one open decision: the completion standard (options in the Assessment Handbook, section 11).
2. Read the Spine Case and the Instructor Guide for Week 1.
3. Verify the items flagged *verify* in the Reading List and the Framework Library, and check current details of any rule, scheme or dataset you will mention.
4. Two weeks before Day 1, send participants the Participant Handbook and Workbook 1, and ask for three candidate problems.
5. Print or share the Case Bank cards for the first week's activities.
6. Recruit two guest panellists for Day 20.

## How the materials fit together

The same day content produces the slides, the instructor notes, the worksheets and the curriculum, so they always agree: Day 7's slides, Day 7 in the Instructor Guide, Worksheet 3.2 in Workbook 3 and Day 7 in the curriculum describe the same session. If you change a day, change it in the course's source files and rebuild (see the repository README), rather than editing one document by hand.

## Conventions

- **Madhyapur is stylised.** Its figures are illustrative and must never be cited as data. Real rules, schemes and institutions are named only where the name is accurate.
- **No primary research.** Participants use published and public evidence only.
- **AI-generated evidence is not evidence.** Every claim must be traced to a source that has been checked.
- **Visual style.** White pages, navy titles, a restrained blue palette, small dots and fine rules, Arial throughout.
