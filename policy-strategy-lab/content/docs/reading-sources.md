## Indian data and documents: where to look

Check the current round, reference year and level of representativeness of every source before using it. Several major surveys are between rounds, and many estimates are valid only at state level.

| Source | Producer | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| National Family Health Survey | Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, with the International Institute for Population Sciences | Health, nutrition, family welfare, household conditions; state and district estimates for some indicators |
| Periodic Labour Force Survey | National Statistics Office, Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation | Employment, participation, wages |
| Household Consumption Expenditure Survey | National Statistics Office | Consumption, living standards |
| UDISE+ | Ministry of Education | School enrolment, teachers, infrastructure, by school and district |
| National Achievement Survey | Ministry of Education | Learning outcomes in sampled grades |
| Annual Status of Education Report | Pratham and the ASER Centre | Foundational reading and arithmetic, rural districts |
| Crime in India and related reports | National Crime Records Bureau | Recorded crime; read the methods notes on recording |
| Census of India | Office of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner | Population, housing, amenities; check the status of the next census |
| Health infrastructure and human resources statistics | Ministry of Health and Family Welfare | Facilities and posts filled, by state |
| Scheme management information systems and dashboards | Implementing ministries | Coverage, outputs, payments; administrative data |
| Output-Outcome Monitoring Framework and Outcome Budgets | Ministry of Finance with NITI Aayog's Development Monitoring and Evaluation Office | Official output and outcome indicators for central schemes |
| National Data and Analytics Platform | NITI Aayog | Harmonised datasets from many sources |
| Open Government Data Platform (data.gov.in) | Government of India | Downloadable datasets from ministries |
| Audit reports | Comptroller and Auditor General of India | Performance and compliance audits by state and sector |
| Social audit reports | State social audit units | Field verification of scheme delivery, especially the rural employment guarantee |
| Standing committee reports | Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha secretariats | Scrutiny of ministries' demands for grants and schemes |
| Bill and budget analyses | PRS Legislative Research | Readable summaries of bills, budgets and state finances |
| Budget analyses | Open Budgets India (Centre for Budget and Governance Accountability) | Union and state budget data |
| State finances | Reserve Bank of India, annual study of state budgets | Fiscal position and spending of states |
| City rankings and urban data | Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (for example, Swachh Survekshan) | Comparative urban service indicators; read the method |
