
## Bibliography

### Primary texts: Sri Aurobindo

All volumes are in The Complete Works of Sri Aurobindo (CWSA), Sri Aurobindo Ashram, Pondicherry. Volume numbers are as commonly listed; confirm against the edition you use.

- The Renaissance in India with A Defence of Indian Culture. CWSA Volume 20. Contains 'The Renaissance in India' (Arya, August to November 1918; revised booklet 1920), 'Indian Culture and External Influence', 'Is India Civilised?' (Arya, December 1918 to February 1919) and 'A Defence of Indian Culture' (Arya, February 1919 to January 1921).
- Collected Plays and Stories. CWSA Volumes 3 and 4. Volume 3: The Viziers of Bassora, Rodogune, Perseus the Deliverer. Volume 4: Eric, Vasavadutta, incomplete plays and stories.
- The Future Poetry. CWSA Volume 26 (verify).
- The Human Cycle, The Ideal of Human Unity, War and Self-Determination. CWSA Volume 25 (verify).
- Essays on the Gita. CWSA Volume 19 (verify).
- Isha Upanishad. CWSA Volume 17 (verify).
- Translations (including The Hero and the Nymph, from Kalidasa's Vikramorvashi). CWSA Volume 5 (verify).
- Early Cultural Writings (including the essays on Kalidasa and on Bankim Chandra). CWSA Volume 1 (verify).
