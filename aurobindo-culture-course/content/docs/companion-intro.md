# Spirit and Form
## Model Readings

This document collects the model readings used in each day's worksheet. Each shows how a careful reader works through the day's text: what the passage says, what it implies, how to judge it, and what to ask next. The model readings are deliberately modest. They are not the right answers; they are examples of the method, and participants are expected to disagree with some of them.

## How to use the model readings

- Read the model reading after you have tried the worksheet yourself, not before.
- Notice the separation of text, inference, judgement and question.
- Check every claim in a model reading against the text in your edition. Where a model reading paraphrases Sri Aurobindo, the paraphrase may be wrong; your edition is the authority.
- Use the model readings in your capstone only as examples of method, never as sources for what Sri Aurobindo wrote.
