# Spirit and Form
## Start Here: the complete course package

A 30-day seminar, two hours a day over six weeks, on Sri Aurobindo's The Renaissance in India, A Defence of Indian Culture and his five complete plays. Each participant carries one question of their own from Day 1 to a capstone essay defended before a panel, and takes part in a staged reading of a scene. This package contains everything needed to run it.

## How to prepare a first run

1. Read the Concept Note and the Text Guide.
2. Read the three set texts in full: CWSA Volume 20 and the five plays in Volumes 3 and 4.
3. **Check every quotation and detail flagged in the materials against the printed texts.** The materials paraphrase wherever possible; a few details of the plays (for example the setting and names in Perseus the Deliverer) are marked for checking.
4. For each day, choose the exact paragraphs for the passage of the day, and the exact lines for the scene of the day, in the edition your participants use.
5. Decide the completion standard (Assessment Handbook, section 8).
6. Two weeks before Day 1, send participants the Participant Handbook, Workbook 1 and the entry diagnostic.
7. Recruit two guest panellists for Day 30, and book a space suitable for staged readings.

## How the materials fit together

The same day content produces the slides, the instructor notes, the worksheets, the curriculum and the activity bank, so they always agree: Day 15's slides, Day 15 in the Instructor Guide, Worksheet 3.5 in Workbook 3 and Day 15 in the curriculum describe the same session. To change a day, change it in the course's source files and rebuild, rather than editing one document by hand.

## Conventions

- **Texts.** Cited from The Complete Works of Sri Aurobindo (CWSA). Cite essay or act and scene, and page.
- **Paraphrase by default.** Quotations appear only where a sentence is famous; every quotation must be checked.
- **Evidence.** AI-generated text is never evidence of what any author wrote.
- **Neutrality.** The course teaches reading and judgement, not doctrine, and is not affiliated with any political or religious organisation.
- **Visual style.** White pages, navy titles, a restrained blue palette, small dots and fine rules, Arial throughout.
